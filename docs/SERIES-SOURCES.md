# Series card sources

The series card data lives in `src/series_facts_data.rs`: one entry per series with name, aliases, developer or publisher (`by`), year of the first game (`since`), number of main games (`games`), 3 or 4 key games and a short `story`.

Rules: facts come from English Wikipedia (and official pages where noted), the story texts are written by us in our own words, nothing is copied. A fact that could not be verified in a source was left out. `games` counts main series games only; it is 0 when no source gives a clear count. `since` is the year of the first game of the series (for Wario and Yoshi the first game with their name, their debut years are in the story). Persona has its own entry, it is not an alias of Megami Tensei. Checked on 2026-11-26.

Translations of the stories (de, es, pt, fr, it, ko, ja, vi, zh) are made from these English texts and add no facts.
