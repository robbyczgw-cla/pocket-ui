Interpretation:
Cold Split and box frames decode PNGs and run Image::resized on the main thread (App::cover).
Box paint asks for the old card-size cover before branching to its box-size cover, decoding twice.
Cabinet already decodes on cabinet-art; its frame work is painting/blitting and cached art.
Pause/quick underlying frames are dominated by Canvas::clear, then text and rectangles (~1-2 ms).
Startup samples include filesystem metadata, allocations, lowercasing names and first glyphs.
No optimization of sub-10 ms timing drifts or the already-fast warm menu raster was attempted.

Benchmark caveat: before this pass POCKETUI_FRAME_BENCH dropped the Quick Settings overlay
after its first frame. Those quick samples time the background. The benchmark now repeats
the overlay too; use latency.sh open.quick/close.quick for a before/after comparison.
