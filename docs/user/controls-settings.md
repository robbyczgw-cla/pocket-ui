# Controls in Settings, setup and on the touch screen

The second half of [Controls](controls.md): navigating Settings, first-start setup, touchscreens, power/volume combos
and controller remapping. For shelf, search, gameplay and pause-menu buttons, see [Controls](controls.md).

## In Settings

Settings opens straight into **Connections**; tap L1/R1 to flip across all eight categories (Connections, Look, Screen &
sound, Controls, Playing, Games, System, Help & tools; see [Open Settings](settings.md#open-settings)). Their icon bar
sits along the top: press Up from the first row to jump into it, switch categories with Left/Right, and drop back in
with Down.

Connections holds Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Tailscale, Remote access (SSH), Sync, Pocket Sync, Web companion and PC streaming
where supported. Select with Up/Down, open with A, return with B.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| D-pad Up / Down | Move through rows (Down wraps from last to first; Up from the top jumps to the category icon bar). |
| On the top icon bar | Left / Right (or L1 / R1) switch categories; Down or A enters the rows; B closes Settings. |
| L1 / R1 | Jump straight to the previous or next category (wraps at ends; tap any icon on touchscreens). |
| HOME | Return directly to Home from almost any Settings page (lists, About, Battery, Lights, Night light, Cozy extras, Sleep & power, Curator). Pages mid-operation (updates, downloads, sign-in, pairing, cleanups, remapping) stay open so nothing aborts; use B there. On Home/shelf, HOME still opens Quick settings. |
| A | Opens rows ending in **›**; resets Screen colour to Neutral; does nothing on Brightness/Volume; toggles or cycles every inline setting (Appearance, Colours, Style, Text size, Motion, Game start animation, Lived-in shelf, Menu sounds, Case sounds, Haptics, On start, Continue where I quit, Console boot screen, Remind me where I was, Screen frames, Over the game, Zoom in the pause menu, Shelf space, Language). |
| D-pad Left / Right | Adjusts inline rows (without **›**, highlighted with **‹ ›**): toggles switches, cycles values, sets Menu sounds/Brightness/Volume level or Screen colour warmth. On the Buttons page, flips Button layout or Button symbols. |
| B | Closes Settings straight back to the shelf or game list you came from. |

From any Settings subpage (Wi-Fi, Tools, Battery, etc.), B always steps back to its category list, even if you jumped
there from a shortcut.

## Touch screen

Touch works right alongside physical controls and mirrors button actions without hiding anything behind touch-only
gestures.

On the shelf, lists, Settings, search and Recent games:

| Touch | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tap a row (or card) | Highlight it; tap again to open or launch. |
| Swipe up / down | Scroll row-by-row (or smoothly inside the manual). |
| Swipe left / right | Step cards in Recent games, or browse along a Cabinet shelf (up/down changes shelves). |
| Tap a bottom button hint (A, B, X, Y, SELECT, START) | Acts just like pressing that button. |
| Tap a keyboard key | Types that letter. |
| Tap a Curator row on a game card (Echoes, Versions, Stickers) | Select, then tap again to open. |

During gameplay, touch routes straight to the game:

- **Nintendo DS:** tap the bottom screen with your finger like a stylus (adapts to stacked or side-by-side layouts; the
  top screen and borders ignore touch). Right stick + R2 still work anytime.
- All other systems ignore touch input.

Quick settings and the power menu support touch even over a paused game (tap rows, arrows and hints). The pause menu and
first-start wizard are button-only for now. Touching keeps the screen awake, though only a physical button wakes a dark
or sleeping screen.

If touch stops responding (for example after a driver restart), pocket-ui automatically rediscovers and reconnects it.
Touches are intentionally ignored during games/apps, while the screen is dark/asleep, on the pause menu/first-start
wizard and over TV out (`touch:` lines in `/var/log/pocket-ui.log` show why, included in Report a problem).

## Power and volume

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| Power, short press | Puts your Pocket to sleep (saves running game first; press Power again to wake). Or switches to **Screen off** (audio keeps playing) if configured that way. |
| Power, held (~1 second) | Opens the **power menu** (won't accidentally sleep on release), or triggers **Sleep** if configured. Also reachable via [Quick settings](quick-settings.md#quick-settings) → **Power…**. |
| Power, held (many seconds) | Hardware forced reboot without saving (last resort if completely frozen). |
| Vol+ / Vol- | Volume (H700 speaker has capped max and physical mute at 0). Hold START + Vol for brightness; SELECT + Vol for colour warmth. |

Sleep triggers on button release so short presses stay distinct from long holds, and the wake press never leaks into a
game or menu. Customise Power button actions under Settings → System → **Sleep & power**
([Sleep & power settings](quick-settings.md#sleep--power-settings)).

### Volume and brightness keys

Available everywhere (shelf, Quick settings and gameplay), with a slim top bar showing the live level:

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| Vol+ / Vol- | Volume |
| START + Vol+ / Vol- | Brightness in 5% steps, continuing below 5% into three [Extra dim](quick-settings.md#extra-dim) levels |
| HOME + Vol+ / Vol- | Brightness via ROCKNIX with pocket-ui's indicator bar and [Extra dim](quick-settings.md#extra-dim) steps |
| SELECT + Vol+ / Vol- | Screen colour warmth: Vol+ warmer, Vol- cooler (**Cooler 1…10**, **Neutral**, **Warmer 1…10**; synced with Settings → Screen & sound → **Screen colour**, where A resets to Neutral) |

RetroArch and standalone emulators apply these changes without drawing pocket-ui's bar; colour warmth and Extra dim
reach RetroArch, PS2, GameCube and PortMaster only where the display supports system-wide gamma. On the TrimUI Brick,
pressing volume keys while the hardware mute slider is engaged displays **Muted** and restores your exact previous volume
when you flip the slider back.

### The power menu

Shows battery charge and time left at top right (matching Settings → System → [Battery](settings-system.md#battery))
with a description under each row:

| Entry | What it does |
|---|---|
| Sleep | Saves your running game and suspends (same as default short press). |
| Restart | Saves your running game, reboots and resumes right back in it. |
| Power off | Saves your running game and shuts down cleanly. |
| Restart pocket-ui | Saves and closes the game, then restarts pocket-ui in a few seconds without rebooting the OS, handy if a menu ever looks stuck. |
| Restart to boot menu | Reboots into the bootloader to switch back to Android (**Switch boot mode** → Android → **Start**). Where unavailable, explains the manual combo (Power off, then hold Vol Down + Power). |

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| D-pad Up / Down | Move. |
| A | Confirm. |
| B, or Power again | Close the power menu and resume gameplay. |

Mid-game, the power menu pauses your game cleanly behind it and restores play if you cancel or wake from Sleep.

### Test buttons

**Settings → Controls → Test buttons** lights up every button, trigger and stick on connected controllers and prints
what each press does in menus and games (stickless handhelds like TrimUI Brick and RG35XX Plus draw without sticks).
Hold HOME for 2 seconds (or B + START together) to exit. See [Test buttons](settings-sound.md#test-buttons).

### Controller setup

Open **Settings → Controls → Controller setup**, or **Pause → Options → Controls → Controller setup** during any
pocket-core game. Pick a controller at the top (A or Left/Right; identical models show an ID suffix plus remembered
player number), highlight an action, press A, then press the desired button or analogue L2/R2 trigger on that pad.

Pressing an already-bound button **swaps** both actions automatically with a confirmation note. Pressing HOME, pressing
built-in controls while remapping an external pad, or waiting 8 seconds cancels safely without changing anything, and
inputs from other controllers are ignored. Press B to exit, or use **Reset controller layout** for the selected pad
only.

On the TrimUI Brick, F1 and F2 are reserved global shortcuts. Pressing them during capture cancels with a reminder to
configure them under Settings → Controls → **Lights & buttons**.

If the active controller disconnects during capture, remapping cancels cleanly without altering your layout, and
replaced input readers release held buttons so nothing ever sticks.

Custom maps follow each controller's ID across reconnects and player changes, applying underneath your Nintendo/Xbox
menu layout and per-game Controls across shelves, menus, pocket-core games and ROCKNIX's SDL Dolphin and ARMSX2.
RetroArch, Azahar (3DS), PortMaster and other standalone players keep their own mappings.

Analogue triggers register at 55% pull and release below 40% to prevent chatter (unchanged analogue triggers keep full
travel in standalone games; digital triggers behave as standard buttons). HOME, power and volume always remain active.
