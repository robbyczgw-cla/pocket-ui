# Retroid Pocket Mini V2

The handheld pocket-ui is tested on most. Run it as **pocket-ui OS** from an SD card, which boots straight to your shelf
and leaves Android untouched, or keep Android and install **pocket-ui for Android**.

- **pocket-ui as:** SD card image, or the [Android app](device-android.md)
- **Status:** Played on
- **Get it:** [install page: Retroid Pocket Mini V2](https://pocketui.xyz/install.html#rp-mini-v2)

## Good to know

- **Starting the card:** hold Volume Down and press Power, set **Boot mode: Loader** and pick **Start**. The choice
  sticks while the card is in; **Boot mode: Android** goes back to Android
  ([Start pocket-ui](getting-started.md#2-start-pocket-ui)).
- **Already on ROCKNIX?** Two files on a fresh ROCKNIX card or the Ports installer add pocket-ui without flashing again
  ([Getting started](getting-started.md#fresh-card-two-files)).
- **Buttons:** the names follow the Mini V2's printed layout, A right and B bottom. Settings → Controls → Buttons →
  **Button layout** swaps them in menus ([Controls](controls.md#button-layout)).
- **Fan:** Settings → Playing → **Performance & fan** sets how fast it runs and how loud the fan gets, in games and in
  menus ([Performance and fan](performance.md)).
- **Games:** from NES to PS2, GameCube, Wii and 3DS. Send them over Wi-Fi from your phone or computer
  ([Add games](getting-started.md#5-add-games)); [Systems](systems.md) has the emulators.
- **Updates:** Settings → System → **Updates** over Wi-Fi, or one zip on the card ([Updates](updates.md)).

## Known limits

- Skip ROCKNIX's own update on this handheld: it also overwrites the bootloader Android needs. Update through pocket-ui
  ([Safe mode](safe-mode.md#opening-rocknixs-own-menu)).
- TV output over USB-C to HDMI isn't tested on hardware yet ([Known limits](troubleshooting.md#known-limits)).
