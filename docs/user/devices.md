# Devices

One short page per handheld: which pocket-ui fits it, how far it is tested, where to get it and what is special about it.
The status words are the ones on the [install page](https://pocketui.xyz/install.html): **Played on** means we play on
that model ourselves, **Beta** and **Experimental** mean it is new there and your reports help. pocket-ui is in alpha on
every handheld, so back up your saves first.

| Handheld | pocket-ui as | Status | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retroid Pocket Mini V2 | SD card, or the Android app | Played on | [Retroid Pocket Mini V2](device-retroid-mini-v2.md) |
| Retroid Pocket 5, Flip 2, Pocket 6, Nova | The Android app (recommended), or an SD card | Card: Beta | [Retroid Pocket 5, Flip 2, 6, Nova and Classic](device-retroid.md) |
| Retroid Pocket Classic | SD card | Experimental | [Retroid Pocket 5, Flip 2, 6, Nova and Classic](device-retroid.md#retroid-pocket-classic) |
| AYN Odin 2, Odin 2 Mini, Odin 2 Portal | SD card, or the Android app | Experimental | [AYN Odin 2 family](device-odin-2.md) |
| Anbernic RG35XX, RG40XX, RG34XX, RG CubeXX, RG28XX, RG-SP | SD card, one per model | RG35XX Plus and SP: Played on | [Anbernic H700](device-anbernic-h700.md) |
| R36S, R36XX | Its own SD card, or the pocket-ui beta for dArkOS | Beta | [R36S and R36XX](device-r36s.md) |
| RK3566 handhelds | One generic SD card | Experimental | [RK3566 generic card](device-rk3566.md) |
| TrimUI Brick, Brick Hammer | Its own card, nothing flashed | | [pocket-ui on the TrimUI Brick](brick-index.md.inc) |
| TrimUI Smart Pro, Brick Pro | The Brick's card | Experimental | [TrimUI Brick Pro and Smart Pro](other-handhelds.md#trimui-brick-pro-experimental) |
| Android handhelds | An app | | [pocket-ui for Android](device-android.md) |

**Not on the list?** An Android handheld with Android 8 or newer runs the app. Already on dArkOS with a 640×480 screen? Try
the pocket-ui beta for dArkOS ([R36S and R36XX](device-r36s.md#pocket-ui-beta-for-darkos)). Not supported: the original
RG35XX (2023, without "Plus" or "2024" on the back), the TrimUI Smart Pro S, and Retroid Pocket 5 or Flip 2 models with
12 GB and the Visionox screen. Anything else: request your handheld on
[GitHub](https://github.com/robbyczgw-cla/pocket-ui/issues) or ask in #help on [Discord](https://discord.gg/sDqUV4M49g).

Card or app? Both give you the same shelf, real boxes and instant Continue; the card boots like a console, the app sits
beside your other Android apps ([Image or app?](https://pocketui.xyz/install.html#card-or-app)). Which consoles run well
where: [Systems](https://pocketui.xyz/systems.html).
