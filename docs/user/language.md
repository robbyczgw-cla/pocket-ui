# Language

Settings → Look & feel → **Language**: menus, hints, messages and the notes over a game are in **English** or
**Deutsch** (German). A or Left/Right on the row switches between them; the screen changes at once,
nothing restarts. The first start's Welcome page has the same choice (Left/Right).

Until you choose, pocket-ui uses the system language set in ROCKNIX's own menu (EmulationStation, in its system
settings): German when that is German, English for every other language. Once you choose here or on the
Welcome page, pocket-ui keeps its own choice, even when the system language changes later.

What stays in English in the German interface:

- **This manual** (Settings → System → **Manual**). Its title line and buttons are German, its pages are not
  translated yet.
- **Names**: games, systems, emulators and cores, people and makers, and pocket-ui's own feature names such as
  Curator, Lens, Echoes and Pixel Path.
- **What comes from elsewhere**: game descriptions from Wikipedia and Wikidata, the facts and notes on the console
  pages, PortMaster's texts, RetroAchievements' achievement names, the names of networks and devices.
- **Technical details** inside a message, such as another program's reply or a missing library name: the
  sentence around them is German.
- **The web companion's pages in the browser** (Settings → Connections → Web companion is German on the Pocket).

A sentence pocket-ui has no German for yet appears in English, never as an empty line.
