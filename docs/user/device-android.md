# pocket-ui for Android

The same shelf, real boxes and instant Continue as an app: keep Android, install one APK.

- **pocket-ui as:** an app (APK)
- **Needs:** Android 8 or newer on a 64-bit ARM device
- **Get it:** [install page: Android](https://pocketui.xyz/install.html#android)

## Good to know

- **Install:** download the APK on your handheld and open it; Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or
  file manager.
- **Your games:** a `ROMs` folder with one folder per system (`ROMs/gba`, `ROMs/snes` …) on internal storage or your SD
  card, BIOS files in `ROMs/bios`. pocket-ui finds every `ROMs` folder and leaves your files where they are.
- **Made for the pad:** with a built-in or connected controller the app opens in Handheld view, with full button control
  ([pocket-ui on your Android handheld](android-index.md.inc)).
- **Emulators built in:** Nintendo DS, PSP, Nintendo 64 and Dreamcast start right away. PS2, GameCube, Wii and 3DS start in
  emulator apps such as ARMSX2, Dolphin or Azahar ([Games in other emulator apps](android-mvp.md#games-in-other-emulator-apps)).
- **Updates:** the shelf tells you when a new version is out, or Settings → System → **Updates**
  ([Android updates](updates.md#android)).

## Known limits

- Android deletes an app's private data when you uninstall it: make a [save backup](android-mvp.md#save-backup) first.
- Android still handles notifications, system bars and battery rules. Settings → System → **Run in background** asks
  Android to let pocket-ui keep working in the background.
- Native Android games on the shelf keep their own saves and don't use save states.
