# Colours and style

Settings → Look → **Colours** and **Style** let you customise your colour palette and menu font, or switch to the full
pixel-art Retro style. Both rows update live: move with Up/Down, press A or Left/Right to cycle choices, and watch the
screen change immediately. Your picks are saved with your look and restored by
[Reset settings](settings-system.md#backup--reset). To change how button prompts look (letters, Xbox colours or
PlayStation shapes), go to [Settings → Controls → Buttons](settings-sound.md#button-symbols).

## Colours

Sets the palette across every screen: highlights, frames, button hints, accents, and, in the Cabinet layout, the wood tone,
shelf nameplates and slot glow. Box art and case artwork always keep their real colours. Every palette has a Dark and
Light variant that follows [Appearance](settings.md#appearance):

- **Warm** (default): paper and walnut.
- **Indigo Cube**: deep indigo with lilac.
- **Grey Brick**: classic grey handheld plastic with dark green.
- **Green X**: bright green on black.
- **Blue Station**: grey plastic with deep blue.

The pause menu, power menu and in-game overlays always stay dark so they don't blind you mid-game (and switch to Retro's
dark palette when Retro style is active).

## Style

If an older version saved the retired Rounded or Pixel style, pocket-ui switches cleanly to Barlow while keeping all your
other settings intact.

Choose the font and menu chrome:

- **Barlow** (default): clean, crisp and compact.
- **Retro**: turns every menu into a classic handheld UI. Sharp pixel text with LCD-style numbers, chunky pixel frames
  around menus, hints and pop-ups, a flat selection bar with a little cursor, colour pixel icons for Settings categories
  and pause rows, and classic blip sounds when [menu sounds](settings-sound.md) are on. Your shelf, box art and
  Continue work identically in every layout. Retro unlocks its own two palettes (each with Dark and Light modes):
  - **Cocoa** (default): warm brown and cream with a tangerine highlight.
  - **Pea**: the iconic four greens of a vintage monochrome screen, including tinted icons.
- **Retro Pixelify**: the same Retro style using the rounder Pixelify font from before 0.3.2 (with LCD-style numbers),
  while standard Retro uses Departure Mono's square pixel letters. Press Right once past Retro to select it.

Titles that run too long shrink or shorten just like in Barlow; wider pixel letters fit slightly fewer characters per
line.
