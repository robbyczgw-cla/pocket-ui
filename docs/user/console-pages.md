# Console pages

Part of [Playing](playing.md#home): dedicated info pages for each console, plus **Credits & links**.

Most consoles have their own hardware page. Highlight a console on Home and look for **X Details** in the hint bar, then press
**X** to open it. *(On a game, X still opens the game's [card](library.md).)*

What you see on the shelf before even opening the page depends on your layout:

| Look | With a system selected |
|---|---|
| **Split** | Shows maker, year and generation under the console name, followed by **Released**, **Ended**, **Sold**, **Inside**, **Your games** (count and last played) and **Runs on** (the active emulator). Missing facts are skipped cleanly; systems without hardware specs (like PC Engine CD) still show Your games and Runs on. On tight screens, lower-priority rows drop first. |
| **Shelf** | Shows maker, year and generation right after the game count on the pulled-out label. |
| **Cabinet** | Systems are shelf rows rather than selectable items, so X on a game opens its card. |

Inside the console page, top to bottom:

- Console name, maker, year, generation, a short overview and hardware artwork ([other colours](settings.md#console-colours))
- **Timeline:** regional launch dates, discontinuation year and lifetime units sold
- **Hardware:** CPU, graphics, RAM, media format, plus screen specs for handhelds
- **Launch price:** original retail prices where verified
- **On this Pocket:** your game count, last-played title, total play time on this system, active emulator and BIOS status
  (found or missing)
- Further down: predecessor and successor consoles, plus notes on what the sales figure covers

| Button | On the console page |
|---|---|
| A | Open that console's games list. |
| B | Return to Home (or to the previous console if you jumped here via [Credits & links](#credits--links)). |
| X | Open [Credits & links](#credits--links): creators, chips and console family. |
| Y | View the full source list for every fact on the page. Press B or Y to return. |
| START | Pick which emulator runs this console: [Runs on](options.md#runs-on). |
| Up / Down, L1 / R1 | Scroll when the page is taller than the screen. |

On touchscreens: tap any hint at the bottom, or swipe up and down to scroll.

All specs come from verified Wikidata, Wikipedia and manufacturer figures stored locally on your Pocket, so console pages
work 100% offline. Anything unverified is left out rather than guessed. Supported consoles: 3DO, 32X, Atari 2600, Atari
7800, Atari Jaguar, Atari Lynx, ColecoVision, Dreamcast, Famicom Disk System, Game Boy, Game Boy Advance, Game Boy Color,
Game Gear, GameCube, Intellivision, Master System, Mega Drive (Genesis), Neo Geo AES, Neo Geo CD, Neo Geo Pocket Color,
Nintendo 3DS, Nintendo 64, Nintendo DS, NES (Famicom), PC Engine (TurboGrafx-16), PlayStation, PlayStation 2, PlayStation
Portable, Sega CD (Mega-CD), Sega Saturn, SG-1000, Super Nintendo (Super Famicom), Vectrex, Virtual Boy, Wii and
WonderSwan. Other categories (Ports, PICO-8, SuperGrafx and add-ons like PC Engine CD) don't have a console page or X
hint.

## Credits & links

Press **X** on any console page to explore who built it, what's inside and jump straight to related consoles and games in
your library:

- A two-sentence summary of who designed it and its hardware
- **People:** key engineers and leads where documented (like Gunpei Yokoi and Satoru Okada for the Game Boy, or Masayuki
  Uemura for the NES and SNES)
- **Made by:** developer and manufacturers
- **Chips:** CPU, graphics and sound chips with their makers, plus other consoles **on this Pocket** sharing the same
  silicon (like the Sharp SM83 across Game Boy, GBC and GBA, or the Zilog Z80 across Master System, Game Gear and Mega
  Drive)
- **Family:** predecessors, successors, hardware revisions (Game Boy Pocket, PS one) and related systems (add-ons, arcade
  boards). Systems you have games for come first as clickable links
- **Best-selling game** with its verified sales figure
- **By the makers, in your library:** up to eight games in your library from the console's own developer or maker (such
  as Nintendo R&D1 on Game Boy), oldest first, plus a count of any more. Right after boot this briefly shows "Getting
  ready…" while game metadata loads.

| Button | In Credits & links |
|---|---|
| Up / Down | Hop between links on screen; keeps scrolling past the last link. |
| L1 / R1 | Jump a full page. |
| A | Follow the selected link to another console page, a system's game list (like PC Engine CD) or a game [card](library.md). |
| B or X | Return to the console page. |

Jump from console to console as deep as you like: B steps back up to eight pages before returning to Home, and B on a game
card brings you right back to Credits & links.

On touchscreens: tap once to highlight a link, tap again to open it, and swipe to scroll.

Every entry is fact-checked against Wikidata or English Wikipedia data (never copied prose) with original summaries and
role notes ("Led the design team") written for pocket-ui. Links only appear for systems that have games on your Pocket.
