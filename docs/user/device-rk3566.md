# RK3566 generic card

One pocket-ui OS card for handhelds with the Rockchip RK3566 chip.

- **pocket-ui as:** one generic SD card image
- **Status:** Experimental, not tested on a real handheld yet
- **Get it:** [install page: RK3566](https://pocketui.xyz/install.html#rk3566). Read the device and installation notes
  beside the download before flashing

## Good to know

- **Some RG353 models** need Android removed from internal storage before this card boots.
- **Plays:** Nintendo DS on DSperate; N64, PSP and Dreamcast on ROCKNIX's emulators ([Systems](systems.md)).
- **Already on dArkOS?** The [pocket-ui beta for dArkOS](device-r36s.md#pocket-ui-beta-for-darkos) runs from Ports on
  640×480 dArkOS handhelds, with no extra card.
- **Updates:** Settings → System → **Updates** over Wi-Fi, or one zip on the card ([Updates](updates.md)).

## Known limits

- Nobody has played this card on real hardware yet: back up your saves first, and tell us how it runs.
